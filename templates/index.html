About this tool

Loadshedding status is retrieved live from the EskomSePush (ESP) API. Municipal fault and maintenance data integration is currently in progress. Data sources in order of priority:

  • Live EskomSePush (ESP) — loadshedding stages (all areas)
  • Live City of Cape Town — electricity faults & planned maintenance
  • Live eThekwini (Durban) — medium voltage electricity faults
  • Live Mangaung / Bloemfontein — Centlec interruption notices
  • Pending City of Johannesburg (City Power) — no public data available
  • Pending City of Tshwane, Nelson Mandela Bay & other metros

The ESP free tier allows 50 requests per day. If this limit is reached, the tool will display a "Data Unavailable" notice until midnight when the counter resets.

This tool checks for known power interruptions that may be affecting your ConnectCo internet connection. For unresolved outages, contact ConnectCo Support.
